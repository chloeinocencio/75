Privacy

75 — effective November 15, 2026

The short version

75 has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your challenge and your progress photos stay on your device. The only thing that ever leaves it is what you choose to share with friends.

Stored only on your device

  • Your challenge — tier, daily checklist, water, reading and meditation logs, rest days, and body weight if you pick 75 Medium.
  • Progress photos. On iPhone they're kept in the app's private storage, encrypted while your phone is locked, and included in your own iPhone backup. On the web they're kept in your browser. 75 never uploads them.
  • Settings — reminder time, units, app lock.

Friends (iPhone, optional)

If you turn on Friends, 75 uses Apple's CloudKit with your iCloud account. It stores:

  • The display name you choose and a random invite code
  • An anonymous, app-specific iCloud identifier (not your Apple ID, email or phone number)
  • While sharing is on: your tier, current day, streak and today's completion percentage
  • Who you've added, so both of you have to add each other before anything is visible

Photos, notes and body weight are never shared. You can turn sharing off, block or report someone, or delete your Friends profile in Settings — deleting removes your profile and connections from iCloud.

Reminders and app lock

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Face ID / Touch ID is handled entirely by iOS; 75 never sees your biometric data.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase all data removes everything 75 stores on your device. Deleting the app does the same. Settings → Delete my Friends profile removes your CloudKit data.

Health

75 is a habit tracker, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a strict diet, intense training or high water intake.

Children

75 isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.
